What we think the job actually is

The sign-in isn't the product. It's the input.

Every free sign-in app's job ends when the visitor taps submit. We think that's where it starts.

A seller report you'd send without editing it

Groups through the door. How many came without an agent. Who was ready and funded. What people actually said about the house, in their words, with names stripped out. Assembled from the sign-ins rather than typed at 9pm on a Sunday.

A clear read on who to ring tonight

Not everyone who signs a sheet is a buyer. Timeline, financing, whether they already have an agent, and whether they went home and opened the flyer again on Wednesday — that's the list worth working, and it's a much shorter list.

Evidence for the price conversation

In week one, 17% of visitors said the price felt high. By week four it's 53%. That's not your opinion against your seller's ego any more — it's a number that moved, collected from people who stood in their kitchen.

Showings counted too, in fifteen seconds

Most feedback already reaches you — a text from the buyer's agent, a call, something said in the driveway. It just never gets written down anywhere it counts. We're not asking anyone to fill in another form. We want the fifteen seconds it takes you to log the text you already got.

What we are not

We don't schedule showings, and we're not a CRM.

ShowingTime schedules. Follow Up Boss and BoldTrail hold your contacts. Both do those jobs better than we ever would, and if you drop either one for us you've made a mistake. We push what we capture into whatever CRM you already use, and we stay out of the way of the rest.
